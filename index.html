    Privacy and network disclosure

    DiveAtlas has no accounts, advertising, cookies, upload endpoint, or remote dive storage. Imports, country inference, and backups are processed only on this device. GoatCounter records aggregate, cookie-free visit statistics but never receives dive records or file contents. GitHub Pages may log visitor IP addresses. Esri, OpenStreetMap, and OpenSeaMap receive requests only when their map layers are displayed; those requests expose your IP address and viewed area but never dive records or file contents.

    This is your Dive Atlas

    DiveAtlas plots your dives on a map and lets you explore the data. Visualize dive profiles and see how a dive stacks up against the rest in terms of depth, duration, tissue gradient, and more.

    DiveAtlas needs two inputs:

    1. Your dives in UDDF format and,
    2. A file that maps dive locations and sites to latitude and longitude.

    Examples of these files can be found here.

    My dive computer is managed with the Shearwater Cloud app, which supports exporting dives as UDDF.

    Once these files are provided, DiveAtlas stores them locally in your browser — no dive files or dive records are ever sent to the internet. (This means you'll need to upload the files again on any new browser.) Map providers receive tile requests as described in the privacy notice.

    Please enjoy, and feel free to submit issues and suggestions here.
